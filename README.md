# forest-resilience-vaia
Reproducible Google Earth Engine workflow deriving summer (July–August) mean time series of Sentinel-2 NDVI and terrain-flattened Sentinel-1 VH backscatter (2017–2026) over Regione Veneto windthrow damage perimeters, aggregated by municipality. Showed that NDVI reliably maps storm damage but overstates ecosystem recovery; VH is being tested to capture structural recovery.
